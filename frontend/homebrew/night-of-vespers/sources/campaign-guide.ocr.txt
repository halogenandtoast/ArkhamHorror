New Keywords: Jinx & Jinxed

@ Investigators will encounter the pervasive and unpredictable effects of
ancient witchcraft throughout the Night of Vespers campaign in the
form of the Jinxand Jinxed keywords.

@ When an investigator fails a fight, evade, or investigate test when
a card with the jinxed keyword is at their location, players at that
location must refer to the current act or agenda and resolve the
forced effect connected to the bolded Jinx text.

‘© Example: During the mythos phase, Sebastian Benitez draws
4 treachery that enters play at hs location with te jinxed
keyword. During the following investigation phase, he takes an Investigate
action at that location and fails. The current agenda features the text
“Jinx: Increase the shroud of your location by 1 until the end of the
round.” Because Sebastian failed an investigate test while the treachery
with the jinxed keyword was in play at his location, his location's shroud
is increased by 1 until the end ofthe round.

@ Ifa card with the jinxed keyword enters play but its effects are not
defined by either the current act or agenda, failed fight, evade, or
investigate tests do not trigger the jinxed keyword.

‘© Example: An enemy with the jinxed keyword enters Elsie
‘Malone's location and engages her. Neither the current actor agenda
define any specific ruling for the jinxed keyword; therefore, if Elsie fails a
‘fight or evade test against this enemy, it wll not trigger any additional
effects

@ The effects of multiple Jinxed cards in play at the same location are

not cumulative unless otherwise specified.

© Example: Simone Toussaint is engaged with 2 enemies with the Jinzed
keyword. The current act has the text Jinx ~ Increase the difficulty of
all tests at this card’s location by 1 until the end of the round. If
Simone fails a fight test against one of those enemies, the difficulty of all
tests performed at her location are increased by 1 until the end of the
