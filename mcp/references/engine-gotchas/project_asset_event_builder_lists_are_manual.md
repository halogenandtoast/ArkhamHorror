---
title: project-asset-event-builder-lists-are-manual
description: "Asset.hs/Event.hs keep allAssets/allEvents as HAND-maintained builder lists; every other card type is generated by cards-discover --builders=. A forgotten entry crashes with \"Unknown asset\" despite def+impl+art existing"
---

`Arkham/Asset.hs` (`allAssets`) and `Arkham/Event.hs` (`allEvents`) enumerate their
`SomeAssetCard`/`SomeEventCard` builders **by hand**, grouped by campaign with
`-- Campaign` / `--- Encounter set [code]` comments. Every other card type
(Enemy, Treachery, Location, Act, Agenda, Story) has its list GENERATED — those modules
are a single `{-# OPTIONS_GHC -F -pgmF cards-discover -optF --builders=<Type> #-}` line
(`Enemy/Enemies.hs`, `Treachery/Treacheries.hs`, ...) and cannot drift.

`Asset/Assets.hs` IS discover-generated, but with `--dir=Assets` (re-exports the modules)
not `--builders=` — the builders are in scope yet still need manual listing. CLAUDE.md's
"cards-discover auto-generates card code registration modules" is only true for the
`--builders=` types; it misleads on assets and events.

Miss an entry and the card def, implementation module, i18n and art can all exist while
`lookupAsset` falls through to `error $ "Unknown asset: " <> show cardCode`
(`Arkham/Asset.hs` ~line 38) — a 500 on first use of the card, not a compile error.
Hit 2026-09-04: Children of Blood registered only 2 of its 6 assets, so Chosen of
Zburamoarte (13093a/b), Sanguine Song (13066), Forged Permit (13067) and Charlie Kane
(13105) were all unreachable.

**How to apply:** after adding an asset or event, grep `Asset.hs`/`Event.hs` for the
builder name — presence of the module under `Asset/Assets/` proves nothing. To audit,
diff each impl module's exported lowercase builders against the registration file; expect
false positives from `*Effect` builders (registered in `Effect.hs`) and standalone
scenarios. Converting both types to `--builders=` would kill this bug class.
