# Q: When I attack using Shrivelling (         60) and reveal a , , , , or 

Q: When I attack using Shrivelling (         60) and reveal a , , , , or 
     symbol, when do I take 1 horror?
     A: You would take 1 horror immediately as you reveal the symbol, during
     “ST.3 Reveal chaos token” (“Skill Test Timing,” Rules Reference, page
     26). If you had any reactions to taking that horror (such as Agnes Baker’s
     ability), you would trigger it then, before resolving the rest of the attack.
