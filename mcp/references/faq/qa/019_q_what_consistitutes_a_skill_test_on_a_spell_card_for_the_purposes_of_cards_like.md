# Q: What consistitutes a “skill test on a Spell card” for the purposes of cards like

Q: What consistitutes a “skill test on a Spell card” for the purposes of cards like
  Spirit Athame ( 35) and Grounded ( 113)?
  A: A “skill test on a card” is any ability that directly prompts a skill test,
  either through the template “test skill (X),” or by initiating an action that is,
  in itself, a skill test (for example, any card with Fight, Evade, or Investigate
  action designators).
