# Q: Can a location with no valid path to my location be the “nearest” or

Q: Can a location with no valid path to my location be the “nearest” or
  “ farthest” location from my location?
  A: Yes, but only if there are no other eligible locations with a vaild path to
  your location. If there are other locations with a valid path to your location,
  the nearest or farthest of those must qualify as the “nearest” or “farthest” to
  your location.
