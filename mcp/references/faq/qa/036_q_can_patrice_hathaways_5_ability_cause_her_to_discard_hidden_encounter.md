# Q: Can Patrice Hathaway’s (         5) ability cause her to discard hidden encounter

Q: Can Patrice Hathaway’s (         5) ability cause her to discard hidden encounter
  cards from her hand?
  A: No. Hidden cards cannot be discarded from hand by any means except
  for those described on the card.
