# Q: Can tokens (like Tony Morgan’s bounties) be placed on swarm cards?

Q: Can tokens (like Tony Morgan’s bounties) be placed on swarm cards?
     A: Yes. Each swarm card counts as a separate copy of its host card and can
     have its own tokens on it.
