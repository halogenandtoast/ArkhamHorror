# Q: As Lola Hayes ( 6), what role do I switch to if I resolve Crisis of Identity

Q: As Lola Hayes ( 6), what role do I switch to if I resolve Crisis of Identity
  ( 19) and the discarded card is a multi-class card? What happens if my deck
  is empty and no card is discarded?
  A: If the discarded card is a multi-class card, you may choose which of those
  classes to switch your role to. If no card is discarded, your role stays the
  same and is not switched.
