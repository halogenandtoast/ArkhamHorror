# Q: If I have Forced Learning ( 31) and I draw a weakness during my

Q: If I have Forced Learning ( 31) and I draw a weakness during my
  upkeep phase, can I choose to discard it? What happens if both cards I draw
  are weaknesses?
  A: Weaknesses cannot be chosen to be discarded from your hand. If you
  draw 1 weakness and 1 non-weakness card, you must choose to discard
  the non-weakness card, then resolve the weakness as normal. If you draw
  2 weaknesses, you cannot discard either one, and must resolve both of them.
