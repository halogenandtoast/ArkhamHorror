# Q: What happens if I am Entombed ( 89) and use Ethereal Form ( 164)

Q: What happens if I am Entombed ( 89) and use Ethereal Form ( 164)
and am already engaged with an enemy? One says I cannot disengage from
enemies, the other says enemies cannot be engaged with me.
A: When two cards directly contradict one another in a way that is
impossible to reconcile, the text of the encounter card takes precedence. In
this case, Entombed would take precedence over Ethereal Form, and the
enemy would remain engaged with you (see section 2.20—The Silver Rule).
