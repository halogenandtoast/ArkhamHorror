# Q: Does Quick Learner (      30) increase the difficulty of skill tests during the

Q: Does Quick Learner (      30) increase the difficulty of skill tests during the
mythos phase?
A: Quick Learner’s ability only affects skill tests during your turn. A skill
test that initiates when your turn begins, or at any point before the
resolution of the first action of your turn, gets +1 difficulty. (Likewise, Plan
of Action ( 24) gains a  and  icon at this same timing point, but not
during the mythos phase).
