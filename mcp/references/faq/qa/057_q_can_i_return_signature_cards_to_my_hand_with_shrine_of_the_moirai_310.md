# Q: Can I return signature cards to my hand with Shrine of the Moirai (         310)?

Q: Can I return signature cards to my hand with Shrine of the Moirai (         310)?
If so, how many levels do they count as?
A: Signature cards do not have a level, so they cannot be returned to your
hand with Shrine of the Moirai.
