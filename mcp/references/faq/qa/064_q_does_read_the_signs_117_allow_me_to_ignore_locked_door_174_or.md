# Q: Does Read the Signs ( 117) allow me to ignore Locked Door (             174) or

Q: Does Read the Signs ( 117) allow me to ignore Locked Door (             174) or
  other effects that would prevent an investigation?
  A:No. Read the Signs only allows you to ignore triggered abilities such as
  the haunted keyword or forced effects on your location. It cannot ignore
  constant effects, such as the one on Locked Door.
