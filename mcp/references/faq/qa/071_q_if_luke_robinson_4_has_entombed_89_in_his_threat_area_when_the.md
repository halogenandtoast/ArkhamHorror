# Q: If Luke Robinson ( 4) has Entombed ( 89) in his threat area when the

Q: If Luke Robinson ( 4) has Entombed ( 89) in his threat area when the
     Forced effect on Dream-Gate (Pointless Reality) ( 15) triggers, what happens?
     A: While Entombed would prevent Luke Robinson from moving, an
     investigator cannot be at “no location” unless explicitly stated. When
     Dream-Gate’s forced effect would move Luke Robinson at the investigation
     phase, you may ignore the effect on Entombed long enough to place Luke in
     a revealed location.
