# Q: On Your Own ( 236) and On Your Own ( 10) are the same level,

Q: On Your Own ( 236) and On Your Own ( 10) are the same level,
  although the latter has the exceptional keyword. Can I upgrade the former into the
  latter version?
  A: Yes, you can upgrade the version of On Your Own without exceptional
  into the version with exceptional by spending 3 experience.
