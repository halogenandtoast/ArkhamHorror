# Q: Can I use the  ability on The Raven Quill ( 42) to mark additional

Q: Can I use the  ability on The Raven Quill ( 42) to mark additional
  checkboxes on its upgrade sheet beyond my deckbuilding access?
  A: No. A customizable card’s level is equal to half the total number of checked
  boxes, rounded up. For example, if your investigator has deckbuilding access
  to Seeker () cards level 0–2, you cannot choose to mark more than 4
  checkboxes on The Raven Quill’s upgrade sheet.
