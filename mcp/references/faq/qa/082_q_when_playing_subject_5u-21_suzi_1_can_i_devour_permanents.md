# Q: When playing Subject 5U-21/“Suzi” ( 1), can I devour permanents,

Q: When playing Subject 5U-21/“Suzi” ( 1), can I devour permanents,
investigator cards, investigator mini-cards, or weaknesses?
A: Suzi cannot devour permanents, investigator cards, or investigator mini-
cards. However, Suzi can devour certain weakness cards, specifically ones
that enter play under their bearer’s control and that don’t have any abilities
preventing them from leaving play.
