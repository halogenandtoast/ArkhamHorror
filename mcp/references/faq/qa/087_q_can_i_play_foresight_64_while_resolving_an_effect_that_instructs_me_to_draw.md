# Q: Can I play Foresight ( 64) while resolving an effect that instructs me to draw

Q: Can I play Foresight ( 64) while resolving an effect that instructs me to draw
multiple cards simultaneously? What about while resolving an effect that lets me
search my deck for a card and draw it?
A: Yes to both. You can play Foresight before drawing multiple simultaneous
cards, and if the card that you name is among the drawn cards, you may
resolve the choice on Foresight for that one card. You can also play Foresight
while resolving an effect that has you search your deck for a card, however, you
must name the card you’re drawing before searching your deck.
