# Q: If a card ability allows me to move damage/horror to another card, can I

Q: If a card ability allows me to move damage/horror to another card, can I
  move that damage/horror to a card without a health or sanity value?
  A: No. An effect that can “move” damage/horror from one card to
  another—such as Wrong Place, Right Time ( 115)—must move that
  damage/horror to a card that has a health or sanity value, respectively.
