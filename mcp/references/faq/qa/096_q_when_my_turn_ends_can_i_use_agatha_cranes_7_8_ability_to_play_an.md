# Q: When my turn ends, can I use Agatha Crane’s ( 7/8)  ability to play an

Q: When my turn ends, can I use Agatha Crane’s ( 7/8)  ability to play an
  event that normally can only be played during my turn?
  A: You resolve Agatha Crane’s ability at the end of your turn, which is still
  during your turn. So you could play an event such as Cryptic Research
  ( 43) with the text “Fast. Play only during your turn.”
