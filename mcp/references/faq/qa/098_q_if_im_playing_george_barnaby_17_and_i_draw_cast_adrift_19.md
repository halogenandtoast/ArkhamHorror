# Q: If I’m playing George Barnaby ( 17) and I draw Cast Adrift (           19)

Q: If I’m playing George Barnaby ( 17) and I draw Cast Adrift (           19)
     during the upkeep phase, when and how is my hand size calculated?
     A: George Barnaby’s first ability is a constant ability. If the number of cards
     beneath George Barnaby changes, your maximum hand size immediately
     changes to the new number.
     If you draw Cast Adrift during step 4.4 of the upkeep phase, you
     immediately discard each card beneath George Barnaby, which sets your
     maximum hand size to 0. You can then trigger George’s  ability to place
     1 discarded card beneath him and draw 1 card, setting your maximum hand
     size to 1. Then at step 4.5 of the upkeep phase, your hand size is equal to
     your current maximum.
