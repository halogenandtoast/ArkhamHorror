# Q: What happens when an enemy with the patrol keyword is already at its target

Q: What happens when an enemy with the patrol keyword is already at its target
     location at the start of the enemy phase?
     A: If a patrol enemy is at the target location indicated by its patrol ability at
     the start of step 3.2 of the enemy phase, that enemy does not move.
