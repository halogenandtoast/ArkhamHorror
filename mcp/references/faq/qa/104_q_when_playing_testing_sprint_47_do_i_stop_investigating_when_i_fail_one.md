# Q: When playing Testing Sprint ( 47), do I stop investigating when I fail one

Q: When playing Testing Sprint ( 47), do I stop investigating when I fail one
     time, or when I finish succeeding or failing to investigate each eligible location?
     A: The former. If you fail one investigate test before you’ve investigated each
     eligible location, you do not continue investigating other locations.
