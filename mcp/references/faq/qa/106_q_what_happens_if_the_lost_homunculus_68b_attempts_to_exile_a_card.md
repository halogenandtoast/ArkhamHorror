# Q: What happens if the Lost Homunculus (         68b) attempts to exile a card

Q: What happens if the Lost Homunculus (         68b) attempts to exile a card
     from their deck?
     A: Exiling a card or removing a card from the game as a cost cannot be
     canceled or ignored. Therefore, the Lost Homunculus will successfully exile
     the card in question. Note that if they do so, they are not able to purchase
     any cards when the scenario ends, and they have no defined “legal deck size”
     to maintain.
