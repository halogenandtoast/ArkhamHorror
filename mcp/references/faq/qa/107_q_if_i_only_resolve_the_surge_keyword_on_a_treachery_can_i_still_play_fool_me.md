# Q: If I only resolve the surge keyword on a treachery, can I still play “Fool me

Q: If I only resolve the surge keyword on a treachery, can I still play “Fool me
  once...” ( 156) and attach the treachery to it?
  A: Yes, resolving the surge keyword on a treachery counts as resolving an
  effect of the card.
