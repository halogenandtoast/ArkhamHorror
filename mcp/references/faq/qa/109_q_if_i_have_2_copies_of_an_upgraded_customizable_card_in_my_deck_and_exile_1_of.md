# Q: If I have 2 copies of an upgraded customizable card in my deck, and exile 1 of

Q: If I have 2 copies of an upgraded customizable card in my deck, and exile 1 of
  them, what would be the experience cost to repurchase that card?
  A: You have two options to repurchase an exiled customizable card: either
  paying the cost of the card as indicated by its upgrade sheet, or if you’re able,
  spending experience on the card’s upgrade sheet to add a copy of that card
  for free to your deck.
