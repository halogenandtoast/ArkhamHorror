# Q: Does Fox Mask ( 67) replenish an offering when an enemy moves with me

Q: Does Fox Mask ( 67) replenish an offering when an enemy moves with me
  (for example, if I am engaged with an enemy and take a move action)?
  A: No. For Fox Mask to replenish an offering, there must be an enemy
  remaining in the location you moved out of.
