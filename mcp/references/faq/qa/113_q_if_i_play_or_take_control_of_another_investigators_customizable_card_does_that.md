# Q: If I play or take control of another investigator’s customizable card, does that

Q: If I play or take control of another investigator’s customizable card, does that
  card retain its upgrades?
  A: Yes. Any upgrades purchased for a customizable card by its owner are
  considered to be printed on that card, regardless of who plays or controls it.
