# Q: If an effect allows me to play a card “at no cost,” does that mean all resource

Q: If an effect allows me to play a card “at no cost,” does that mean all resource
  costs, additional costs, etc. are ignored?
  A: No. Playing a card “at no cost” only ignores its resource cost. Any
  additional costs applied to the card must still be paid.
