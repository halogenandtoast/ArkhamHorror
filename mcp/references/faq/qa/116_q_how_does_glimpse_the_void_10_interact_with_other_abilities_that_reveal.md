# Q: How does Glimpse the Void (         10) interact with other abilities that reveal

Q: How does Glimpse the Void (         10) interact with other abilities that reveal
  tokens?
  A: Glimpse the Void causes you to reveal and resolve 1 additional token on
  top of any you would normally reveal and resolve by/for the test or effect.
  This token is targeted, chosen, or ignored separately from the other token(s)
  revealed by/for the test or effect.
