# Q: If I am instructed to replay a scenario, do any effects from a previous

Q: If I am instructed to replay a scenario, do any effects from a previous
  resolution or interlude that refer to “the next scenario” apply? What about effects
  from the introduction that refer to “this scenario?”
  A: Yes to both. If the previous resolution or interlude affects “the next
  scenario,” or if the scenario introduction affects “this scenario,” it should
  be construed to mean any playthrough of that scenario, including multiple
  playthroughs if the players are forced to replay it.
