# Q: In “The City of Archives,” if I am instructed to use the Body of a Yithian

Q: In “The City of Archives,” if I am instructed to use the Body of a Yithian
  ( 244) for the remainder of the campaign, how should I set up my investigator
  in future scenarios?
  A: In future scenarios, you must swap your investigator card with a Body
  of a Yithian card during Step 10 of Setting Up the Game, as outlined in the
  Rules Reference, just as you would when playing “The City of Archives”
  scenario (see above).
