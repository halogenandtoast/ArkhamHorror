# Q: In “The Wages of Sin”, When a location flips from its non-Spectral side to

Q: In “The Wages of Sin”, When a location flips from its non-Spectral side to
     its Spectral side (or vice versa), what happens to tokens, attachments, or other
     cards at that location?
     A: When such a location flips from one side to its other side, the location
     does not leave play. Therefore, all tokens that were on that location
     remain on its new side, all attachments remain attached, and all enemies,
     investigators and assets that were at that location remain as well.
