# Q: Can I use a card effect that discards a host enemy without defeating it, such as

Q: Can I use a card effect that discards a host enemy without defeating it, such as
     Close Call ( 83)? If so, what happens to its swarm cards?
     A: A host enemy cannot be defeated while it still has swarm cards underneath
     it, but it can still be discarded or removed through other methods. In such a
     case, its swarm cards would also leave play.
