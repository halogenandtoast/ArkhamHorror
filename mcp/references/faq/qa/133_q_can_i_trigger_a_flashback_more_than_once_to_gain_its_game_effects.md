# Q: Can I trigger a Flashback more than once to gain its game effects

Q: Can I trigger a Flashback more than once to gain its game effects
     multiple times?
     A: No. Generally speaking, unless otherwise noted, interludes and
     other sections in the campaign guide (such as Flashbacks) are only ever
     resolved once.
