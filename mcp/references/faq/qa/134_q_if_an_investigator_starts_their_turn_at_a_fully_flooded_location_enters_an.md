# Q: If an investigator starts their turn at a fully flooded location, enters an

Q: If an investigator starts their turn at a fully flooded location, enters an
     unrevealed location with no flood token, and that location becomes flooded “after
     it is revealed,” does the investigator still “struggle for air?” Or does this count as
     entering an unflooded location?
     A: Technically the investigator has entered an unflooded location, which then
     became flooded after it was revealed, so they would not struggle for air.
