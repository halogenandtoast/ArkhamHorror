# Q: How many concealed mini-cards can I expose if I resolve an ability with

Q: How many concealed mini-cards can I expose if I resolve an ability with
  multiple effects, such as Power Word ( 81) with its Tonguetwister or Thrice
  Spoken commands, or Breaking and Entering ( 114)?
  A: An ability that contains multiple effects able to expose an enemy can
  have just one of those effects replaced with the exposure. A concealed mini-
  card may be exposed by successfully attacking it, successfully/automatically
  evading it, successfully investigating its location, automatically dealing
  damage to it, or automatically discovering a clue at its location. Any of these
  effects can be replaced with the exposing of one mini-card, and only one
  mini-card at a time can be exposed by such an effect.
