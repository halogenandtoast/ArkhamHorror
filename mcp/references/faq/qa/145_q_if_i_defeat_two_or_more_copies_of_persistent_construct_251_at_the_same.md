# Q: If I defeat two or more copies of Persistent Construct ( 251) at the same

Q: If I defeat two or more copies of Persistent Construct ( 251) at the same
time, does the Forced effect on one of them bring the other back into play?
A: No. If you use an effect that defeats multiple enemies at once by a card
such as Storm of Spirits ( 153) or Dynamite Blast ( 24), each defeated
Persistent Construct is defeated simultaneously, and thus cannot be
retrieved from the victory display via the Forced effect of another.
