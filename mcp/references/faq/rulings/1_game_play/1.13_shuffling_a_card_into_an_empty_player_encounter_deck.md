# (1.13) Shuffling A Card Into An Empty Player/Encounter Deck

A single card cannot be shuffled into an empty player deck or encounter
deck via card effect. If this shuffling would occur during the playing or
revelation of a card that is typically discarded after it is resolved, such as an
event or treachery card, it is discarded. Otherwise, the card remains in its
current game area.
