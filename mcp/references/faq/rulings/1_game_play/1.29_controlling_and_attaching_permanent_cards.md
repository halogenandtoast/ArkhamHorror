# (1.29) Controlling and Attaching Permanent Cards

A card with the permanent keyword cannot leave its controller’s play area
unless directed by scenario card effects. Cards with the permanent keyword
cannot be attached to other cards in an investigator’s play area. Investigators
cannot take control of another investigator’s permanent cards.
For example: an investigator cannot attach Sin-Eater (      94) to Elle Rubash
( 92) even if Sin-Eater has one or more doom on it.
