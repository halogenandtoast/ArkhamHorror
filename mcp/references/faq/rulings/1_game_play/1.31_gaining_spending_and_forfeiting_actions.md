# (1.31) Gaining, Spending, and Forfeiting Actions

Investigators gain actions during step 4.2 of the upkeep phase. Any unused
actions are forfeited at the end of an investigator’s turn (during step 2.2.2
of the investigation phase). An investigator may lose actions via card effects
outside of their turn, but may not voluntarily spend actions outside of their
turn unless explicitly granted via a card effect.
Note: Investigators always begin the game with 3 actions.
