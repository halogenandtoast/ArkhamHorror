# (1.35) Weaknesses with Player Cardtypes

Unlike other player cards that are not affiliated with any class, weakness
  cards with player cardtypes (asset, event, or skill) are not considered to be
  Neutral cards. All weaknesses, regardless of their primary cardtype, do not
  interact with the player card class system or an investigator’s Deckbuilding
  Options.
