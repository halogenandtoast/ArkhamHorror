# (1.36) Scenario Effects on Investigator Decks

A scenario may instruct an investigator to add a card to their deck. Unless
  this instruction includes the word “may,” the investigator must add that card
  to their deck; this effect cannot be canceled or ignored, and is not restricted
  by an investigator’s Deckbuilding Options.
