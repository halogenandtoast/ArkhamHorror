# (1.3) Reaction () Opportunities

When a triggering condition resolves, investigators are granted the
  opportunity to resolve  abilities in response to that triggering condition.
  It is only after all investigators have passed their reaction opportunity that
  the game moves forward.
  Using a  ability in response to a triggering condition does not prevent other
   abilities from being used in response to that same triggering condition.
  For example: Roland has just defeated an enemy and wishes to trigger his
   ability: “After you defeat an enemy: Discover 1 clue at your location.” He
  discovers 1 clue at his location. He may then play Evidence! ( 22) in response
  to defeating that same enemy. As both cards have the same triggering condition
  (“After you defeat an enemy”), triggering one of these reactions does not prevent
  Roland from triggering the other.
