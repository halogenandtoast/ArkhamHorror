# (1.7) Skill Test Results and Advanced Timing

During Step 7 of Skill Test Timing (“Apply skill test results”), all of the
  effects of the successful skill test are determined and resolved, one at a time.
  This includes the effects of the test itself (such as the clue discovered while
  investigating, or the damage dealt during an attack), as well as any “If this test
  is successful…” effects from card abilities or skill cards committed to the test.
   or Forced abilities with a triggering condition dependent upon the
  skill test being successful or unsuccessful (such as “After you successfully
  investigate,” or “After you fail a skill test by 2 or more”) do not trigger at this
  time. These abilities are triggered during Step 6, “Determine success/failure
  of skill test.”
