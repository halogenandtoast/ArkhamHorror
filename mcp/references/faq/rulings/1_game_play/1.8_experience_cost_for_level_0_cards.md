# (1.8) Experience Cost for Level 0 Cards

When purchasing a new card during campaign play, an investigator must
  pay a minimum of 1 experience. As a result, level 0 cards cost 1 experience
  to purchase. This minimum only applies when purchasing new cards. It does
  not permanently alter a card’s level or experience cost, and does not apply
  when upgrading a card to a higher level version.
