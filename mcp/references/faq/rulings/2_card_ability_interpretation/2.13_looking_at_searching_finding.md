# (2.13) “Looking at,” “Searching,” & “Finding”

These three terms all involve sifting through out-of-play cards (typically an
  investigator’s deck or the encounter deck), but do not count as one another
  for the purposes of other card effects. For example, if an investigator “looks
  at” the top 3 cards of their deck, this is not a search effect. Additionally, while
  “finding” a card does typically involve searching through out-of-play areas, this is
  also not considered to be a search effect.
