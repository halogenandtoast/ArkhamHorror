# (2.14) “Draw” vs “Add to hand”

If a card explicitly adds a card to an investigator’s hand without using the
  word “draw,” it does not count as “drawing” a card for the purposes of other
  card effects. (Note that some abilities such as relevation abilities on weaknesses
  and Dilemma cards trigger regardless of whether the card is “drawn” or “added”
  to your hand.)
