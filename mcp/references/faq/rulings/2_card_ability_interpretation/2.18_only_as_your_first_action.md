# (2.18) “…only as your first action”

If a card can only be played or its ability only triggered as your “first action,”
     it must spend one of your actions, and that action must be the first one you
     have taken during your turn. If it is fast, a free triggered ability, ignores
     its action cost, or takes place outside of your turn, it cannot be played or
     triggered.
