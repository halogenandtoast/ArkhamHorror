# (2.21) Treachery and Event Subtitles

Treachery and event cards do not have a header for their subtitle. If the title
     of a treachery or event card contains 1 or more words in parentheses, that is
     considered to be its subtitle. Other cards with the same title and a different
     subtitle are still considered to be copies of one another. e.g. Restless Journey
     (Fallacy) and Restless Journey (Hardship) are both copies of Restless Journey.
