# (2.28) Memories of Another Life

Memories of Another Life ( 125) can only search the collection for a
  chosen card. It cannot search any in-play or out-of-play area involved in the
  current game, and it cannot create a new copy of a card that does not exist
  physically within your collection.
  Memories of Another Life cannot search for a copy of a customizable card
  the committing investigator has already upgraded, as such a card is no
  longer level 0 for that investigator. It can play a level 0 card that has been
  Chained via the List of Taboos.
  A card played by Memories of Another life can be placed in the victory
  display, or any other eligible out-of-play area, by an ability or game effect.
