# Constant Ability

A constant ability is a type of card ability.
  A constant ability is always interacting with other game elements as long as
  the card it is printed on is in play.
  • Some constant abilities require a specific condition to be met. These
      abilities are denoted by words such as “during” or “while,” and the
      effects of such abilities are active whenever the specified condition
      is met.
  • Constant abilities do not trigger at any specific point.
