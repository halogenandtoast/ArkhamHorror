# Encounter Deck

The encounter deck contains the encounter cards (enemy, treachery, and
  other cards specified by the scenario) the investigators must face during the
  current scenario.
  • If the encounter deck is empty, shuffle the encounter discard pile back
      into the encounter deck.
  • If the encounter deck runs out of cards while resolving a card ability
      or game effect, after resolving that effect in full, shuffle the encounter
      discard pile back into the encounter deck.
  See “Deck” on page 8.
