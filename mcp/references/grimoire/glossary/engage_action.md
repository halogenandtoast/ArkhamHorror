# Engage Action

“Engage” is an action an investigator may take during their turn in the
investigation phase.
To engage an enemy at the same location, an investigator places the
chosen enemy in their threat area. The investigator and the enemy are now
engaged. (For example, this might be done to engage an exhausted enemy, an
aloof enemy, or an enemy engaged with another investigator.)
• An investigator may perform the engage action to engage an enemy
    that is engaged with a different investigator at the same location. The
    enemy simultaneously disengages from the previous investigator and
    engages the investigator performing the action.
• An investigator cannot use the engage action to engage an enemy they
    are already engaged with, or an enemy with the massive keyword.
• An engage action provokes attacks of opportunity.
