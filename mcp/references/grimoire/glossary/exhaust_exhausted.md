# Exhaust, Exhausted

A card that is rotated 90 degrees sideways is considered to be exhausted.
  A card can become exhausted either as part of an ability cost or other game
  effect. When a card exhausts, it is rotated 90 degrees (sideways).
  • An exhausted card cannot exhaust again until it is readied (typically
      during the upkeep phase or via a card ability).
