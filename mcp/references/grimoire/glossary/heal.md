# Heal

“Heal” is an instruction to remove the indicated amount of damage or the
  indicated amount of horror from a card.
  • If a card is healed for more damage or horror than it currently has on
      it, remove as much of the indicated amount as possible. Any remaining
      damage/horror that was not healed is considered to have been healed
      “in excess” of the card’s maximum health/sanity.
  • Moving damage and/or horror from one card to another is not
      considered to be healing for the purposes of card effects, nor is it
      considered to be “damaging” them.
