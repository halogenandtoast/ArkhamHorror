# Investigation Phase

The investigation phase is the second phase of each round. In it,
investigators each take a turn in any order, during which they perform up
to 3 actions (by default).
• If a card effect causes an investigator to gain or lose actions outside
    of the investigation phase, those actions are gained or lost when that
    investigator’s turn begins during the investigation phase.
