# Myriad

Myriad is a deckbuilding keyword.
  An investigator may include up to three copies of a player card with the
  myriad keyword in their deck (by title), instead of the normal limit of two
  copies. Additionally, when an investigator purchases a myriad card for their
  deck, they may purchase up to two additional copies of that card (at the
  same level) at no experience cost.
