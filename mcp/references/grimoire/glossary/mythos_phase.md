# Mythos Phase

During the Mythos phase, investigators place 1 doom on the agenda. If
  the agenda reaches its doom threshold, investigators advance the agenda,
  resolving the text on its reverse (“b”) side.
  Afterward, in player order, each investigator draws the top card of the
  encounter deck.
  See “Mythos phase” on page 27.
