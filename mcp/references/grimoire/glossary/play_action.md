# Play Action

“Play” is an action an investigator may take during their turn in the
  investigation phase.
  When an investigator takes this action, that investigator selects an asset or
  event card in their hand, pays its costs, and plays it.
  • A play action provokes attacks of opportunity unless it is also another
      type of action that does not provoke an attack (i.e. Fight, Evade, etc.).
  • Cards with the “fast” keyword are not played by using this action, and
      may have other timing windows and restritions.
  • When an ability allows a player to “play” a card (typically at a reduced
      cost or in addition to other effects), this also does not use the play action
      and therefore does not provoke attacks of opportunity.
  • Skill cards are not “played.” These cards are committed to a skill test
      from a player’s hand in order to use their abilities.
  See “Fast” on page 12, “Play” on page 18, and “Put Into Play” on page 19.
