# Resign

Some abilities are identified with a Resign action designator. Such abilities
  are initiated using the “Activate” action.
  • An investigator who resigns is eliminated from the scenario, but is not
      considered to have been defeated. (Thus, resigned investigators are not
      required for abilities that require “each surviving investigator.”)
  • Taking a resign action does not provoke attacks of opportunity.
  See “Activate Action” on page 4 and “Elimination” on page 10.
