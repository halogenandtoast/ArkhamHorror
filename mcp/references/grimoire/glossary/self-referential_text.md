# Self-Referential Text

When a card’s ability text refers to its own title, it is referring to itself only,
  and not to other copies (by title) of the card.
  Self-referential abilities using the word “this” (for example, “this card”)
  refer only to the card on which the ability is located, and not to copies of
  that card.
