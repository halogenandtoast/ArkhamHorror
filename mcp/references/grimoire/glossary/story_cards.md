# Story Cards

Story card is a cardtype. These cards serve as an avenue for additional
 narrative and often appear as the reverse side of another scenario card.
 When instructed to resolve a story card, simply read its story text and
 resolve its game text, if any.
 • Flipping and resolving a card with story text on its reverse side does
     not count as “flipping” for the purposes of card effects. Any text on
     the front side of a story card is still considered active, and no tokens,
     attachments, or other game elements are changed until all the story text
     on the reverse side has been resolved.
 See “Cardtypes” on page 6.
