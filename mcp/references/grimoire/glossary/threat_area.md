# Threat Area

An investigator’s threat area is a play area in which encounter cards
currently engaged with and/or affecting an investigator are placed.
• The cards in an investigator’s threat area are at the same location as the
    investigator.
   = Unless otherwise stated, resolving an “as if ” ability does not
      inherently alter the contents of an investigator’s threat area.
      (See “‘As If ’” on page 4.)
• Investigators are permitted to use triggered abilities on scenario cards
    in the threat areas of any investigator at their location.
