# After

The word "after" refers to the moment immediately after the specified
timing point or triggering condition has fully resolved.

*(For example, an ability that reads "After you draw an enemy card"
initiates immediately after resolving all of the steps for drawing an
enemy – resolving its revelation ability, spawning it, etc.)*

See also: "[Ability](#Ability)" on page 2, "[Priority of Simultaneous
Resolution](#Priority_of_Simultaneous_Resolution)" on page 17.
