# Deck

There are 4 main types of decks that appear in any game: the
Investigator Deck, the Encounter Deck, the Act Deck, and the Agenda
Deck.

- The order of cards within a deck may not be altered unless a player is
  instructed to do so by a card ability.

See also: "[Investigator Deck](#Investigator_Deck)" on page 13,
"[Encounter Deck](#Encounter_Deck)" on page 10, "[Act Deck and Agenda
Deck](#Act_Deck_and_Agenda_Deck)" on page 3.
