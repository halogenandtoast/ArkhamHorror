# Doubt and Conviction

Some story resolutions and interludes in *The Path to Carcosa* campaign
instruct the players to "Mark one **Doubt**" or "Mark one
**Conviction**" in their Campaign Log. This is done by filling in one of
the boxes next to "**Doubt**" or "**Conviction** at the bottom of the
Campaign Log.

Later in the campaign, some scenarios may be changed or altered
depending on whether the investigators "have more **Doubt** than
**Conviction**" or "have more **Conviction** than **Doubt**." The
investigators have more **Doubt** than **Conviction** if the number of
boxes filled in next to **Doubt** is greater than the number of boxes
filled in next to **Conviction** (and vice versa). **Doubt** and
**Conviction** are shared among all of the investigators, and they are
not tied to any specific investigator. **Doubt** and **Conviction** have
no game effect except when explicitly referenced by the Campaign Guide
or by a card effect.
