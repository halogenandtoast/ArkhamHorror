# Enters Play

The phrase "enters play" refers to any time a card makes a transition
from an out-of-play area into a play area (see "[In Play and Out of
Play](#In_Play_and_Out_of_Play)" on page 13).

- If an ability (either on the card itself or from another card) causes
  a card to enter play in a state different from that specified by the
  rules, there is no transition to that state. It merely enters play in
  that state.
