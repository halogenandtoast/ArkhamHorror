# Exhaust, Exhausted

Occasionally, a card ability or game step will cause a card to exhaust
to indicate it has been used to perform a function. When a card
exhausts, it is rotated 90 degrees. A card in this state is said to be
exhausted.

- An exhausted card cannot exhaust again until it is ready (typically by
  a game step or card ability).
