# "Farthest from all investigators"

Some card effects instruct investigators to put a card into play at the
location farthest from all investigators. This is determined by finding
the location from which the shortest distance (by movement) to any
investigator is the highest out of all eligible locations. In the event
of a tie, as usual, the lead investigator decides.

*For example: Location A is 5 connections away from investigator A and 1
connection away from investigator B. Location B is 3 connections away
from investigator A and 4 connections away from investigator B. Location
B is therefore the farthest from both investigators, because its
distance to the nearest investigator is greater than that of location A.
(This is true even though location A is farther from investigator A.)*
