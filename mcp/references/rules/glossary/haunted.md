# Haunted

Haunted is a new ability that appears on some locations. Each time an
investigator fails a skill test while investigating a location, after
applying all results for that skill test, that investigator must resolve
all "**Haunted** –" abilities on that location.

A location is "haunted" for the purposes of other card effects if it has
at least one "**Haunted** –" ability (printed or otherwise).
