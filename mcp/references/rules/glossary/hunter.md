# Hunter

Hunter is a keyword ability.

During the enemy phase (in [framework step 3.2](#Enemy_Phase)), each
ready, unengaged enemy with the hunter keyword moves to a connecting
location, along the shortest path towards the nearest investigator.
Enemies at a location with one or more investigators do not move.

- If there are multiple equidistant investigators who qualify as "the
  nearest investigator," the enemy moves towards the one of those who
  best meets its prey instructions. If none do, or if the enemy has no
  prey instructions, the lead investigator may choose an investigator
  for the enemy to move towards.
- If a hunter enemy would be compelled to a location to which the move
  is blocked by a card ability, the enemy does not move.

See also: "[Prey](#Prey)" on page 17.
