# Move Action

"Move" is an action an investigator may take during his or her turn in
the investigation phase.

When an investigator takes this action, move that investigator (using
his or her mini card) to any other location that is marked as a
connecting location on his or her current location (see "[Move](#Move)"
on page 15).
