# Mulligan

After a player draws a starting hand during setup, that player has a
single opportunity to declare a mulligan on any number of the drawn
cards he or she does not wish to keep in his or her starting hand. These
cards are set aside, and an equivalent number of cards are drawn and
added to the player's starting hand. The set-aside cards are then
shuffled back into the player's deck.

- Players take or forgo the opportunity to mulligan in player order.
