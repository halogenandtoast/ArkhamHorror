# Ownership and Control

A card's owner is the player whose deck (or game area) held the card at
the start of the game.

A player controls the cards located in his or her out-of-play game areas
(such as the hand, deck, discard pile).

The scenario controls the cards in its out-of-play game areas (such as
the encounter, act, and agenda decks, and the encounter discard pile).

- Cards by default enter play under their owner's control. Some
  abilities may cause cards to change control during a game.
- If a card would enter an out-of-play area that does not belong to the
  card's owner, the card is physically placed in its owner's equivalent
  out-of-play area instead. The card is considered to have entered its
  controller's out-of-play area, and only the physical placement of the
  card is adjusted.
