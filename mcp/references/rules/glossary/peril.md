# Peril

Peril is a keyword ability.

While resolving the drawing of an encounter card with the peril keyword,
an investigator cannot confer with the other players. Those players
cannot play cards, trigger abilities, or commit cards to that
investigator's skill test(s) while the peril encounter is resolving.
