# Play Action

"Play" is an action an investigator may take during his or her turn in
the investigation phase.

When an investigator takes this action, that investigator selects an
asset or event card in his or her hand, pays its resource cost, and
plays it (see "[Play](#Play)" on page 16).

- Cards with the "fast" keyword are not played by using this action (see
  "[Fast](#Fast)" on page 11).
- Skill cards are not "played." These cards are *committed* to a skill
  test from a player's hand in order to use their abilities.
