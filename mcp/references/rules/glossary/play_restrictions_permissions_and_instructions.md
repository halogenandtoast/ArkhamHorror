# Play Restrictions, Permissions, and Instructions

Many cards and abilities contain specific instructions pertaining to
when or how they may or may not be used, or to specific conditions that
must be true in order to use them. In order to use such an ability or to
play such a card, its play restrictions must be observed.

A permission allows a player to play a card or use an ability outside
the timing specifications provided by the game rules.

A play instruction describes the timing point at which, and/or time
period during which, an event card may be played.
