# Put into Play

Some card abilities cause a card to be "put into play." Such abilities
place the card directly into play from an out-of-play state.

- The resource cost of a card being put into play **is not paid**.
- Unless otherwise stated by the put into play ability, cards that enter
  play in this manner must do so in a play area that satisfies the
  standard game rules associated with playing or drawing (for encounter
  cards) that card.
- A card that has been put into play is not considered to have been
  played or drawn.
