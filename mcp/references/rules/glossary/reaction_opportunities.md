# Reaction Opportunities

When a triggering condition resolves, investigators are granted the
opportunity to resolve \[reaction\] abilities in response to that
triggering condition. It is only after all investigators have passed
their reaction opportunity that the game moves forward.

Using a \[reaction\] ability in response to a triggering condition does
not prevent other \[reaction\] abilities from being used in response to
that same triggering condition.

*For example: Roland has just defeated an enemy and wishes to trigger
his \[reaction\] ability: “After you defeat an enemy: Discover 1 clue at
your location.” He discovers 1 clue at his location. He may then play
Evidence! in response to defeating that same enemy. As both cards have
the same triggering condition (“After you defeat an enemy”), triggering
one of these reactions does not prevent Roland from triggering the
other.*
