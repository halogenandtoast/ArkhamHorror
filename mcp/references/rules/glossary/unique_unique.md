# Unique (\[unique\])

A card with the \[unique\] symbol before its card title is a unique
card. There can be no more than one instance of each unique card, by
title, in play at any given time.

- A player cannot bring into play a unique card if a copy of that card
  (by title) is already in play.
- If a unique encounter card that shares a title with a unique player
  card would enter play, discard the player card simultaneously as the
  encounter card enters play.
